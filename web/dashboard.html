Automated encoder repeatability · raised wheels only

Before enabling in firmware, verify rated motor voltage at TB6612 VM (many TT motors must NOT receive a full 2S battery voltage). Keep wheels secured above ground and a physical motor-power switch reachable.

With the standard Arm step and acknowledgement below, each Auto test runs exactly 3 brief 350ms PWM pulses, one motor at a time, with 1s cooldowns. The rover stops on lost browser heartbeat or STOP. This measures pulses per timed pulse, not pulses per 360° wheel turn.
